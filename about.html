ABOUT

Practical technology guides for everyday life.

IT Assistant publishes clear, calm technology guidance for people who want to solve everyday problems safely.

Our approach

We explain the simple checks first, avoid unnecessary downloads and point readers toward official help when it is useful. Guides are educational and cannot replace support from a device maker, employer, school or qualified professional.

Editorial independence

Recommendations are written to help readers make safer decisions. If the site uses advertising or affiliate links in the future, they will be clearly separated from editorial content.
