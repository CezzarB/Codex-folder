DEVICES & ECOSYSTEMS

Windows, Apple or Android?
Choose what fits you.

The best platform is the one that works with the devices you already own and the tasks you do every day—not the one with the loudest advertising.

QUICK COMPARISON

Three ecosystems. Different strengths.

⊞

WINDOWS

Best for flexibility

Often a strong choice for a wide range of PC hardware, familiar work software, gaming and people who want more options when choosing a computer.

Choose Windows if:
  • You need specific PC software or peripherals.
  • You want many laptop and desktop choices.
  • You play PC games or prefer customisation.
●

APPLE

Best for an all-Apple setup

Mac, iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch are designed to work closely together through features such as Continuity and Handoff.

Choose Apple if:
  • You already use and enjoy an iPhone or iPad.
  • You value a consistent experience across devices.
  • The apps you need are available on macOS and iOS.
◇

ANDROID

Best for phone choice

Android offers a large variety of phones, tablets and brands, with more ways to personalise devices and choose a price range.

Choose Android if:
  • You want lots of device and price options.
  • You like customisation and Google services.
  • You want to compare brands instead of one manufacturer.
Important: Apple and Android are primarily mobile ecosystems, while Windows is a computer operating system. Many people happily use a Windows PC with either an iPhone or an Android phone.

Use this simple decision guide

Already own an iPhone, Mac and iPad?Apple can feel seamless because its devices share work through Continuity features.
Need a PC for work, a specific program or games?Start by checking whether the program or hardware needs Windows, then choose a computer that meets those requirements.
Buying a phone with a limited budget?Compare Android devices from several brands, their support period, camera, battery and storage—not just the headline specifications.
Want to mix devices?That is fine. Focus on the apps you use, cloud storage, browser and password manager so your files remain accessible everywhere.

Before buying, write down your three most important tasks and check the exact apps, accessories and support period for the specific device—not just the platform name.
