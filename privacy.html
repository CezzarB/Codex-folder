Privacy policy

Last updated: August 2026

IT Assistant is an informational website. We do not require visitor accounts. If you use a contact or Digital Checkup form, we receive the name, email address, topic, message and preferred time you choose to send. We use that information only to respond to your message or follow up on your request.

Analytics cookies

With your permission, this site uses Google Analytics to understand general site usage, such as which pages are visited and which device types are used. This helps us improve the guides. Google Analytics is not loaded until you select “Accept analytics” in the cookie notice.

You can decline analytics without affecting your use of the site. To change a previous choice, clear this site’s cookies or local storage in your browser, then reload the page.

Hosting and third-party services

This site is hosted by Netlify. Netlify may process technical information such as IP address, browser type and server logs to deliver and secure the website. Google Analytics processes data according to Google’s privacy practices. These services have their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. For privacy questions, contact contact@it-assistant.org.
